# Part A draft (scratch, not committed)

Rough first guess at Cortex's loop, by instinct, before the lecture.

## 1. What makes Cortex start?
Every Monday at 9am before the VP sync, and also whenever a new message or PRD arrives.

## 2. What does "done" mean for one run?
Status update drafted and queued for my review, nothing sent.

## 3. When should it stop or hand off to a human?
- Stop if it does not have the full content.
- Stop if it does not find the correct data it needs.
- Stop if the updates seem contradictory in email versus Slack.
- Stop if it requires a lot of guesswork on things that have not been decided, as to who the owner is or when the deadline is.
- Stop if there is no clear measure of success, and so we do not know what data to even begin looking for.

## 4. What does Cortex need to do the job?
- Google Docs
- Slack
- Email
- The product admin dashboard

## Question I'm least sure about
How does Cortex know that it is stuck, or that it is on the right path?
